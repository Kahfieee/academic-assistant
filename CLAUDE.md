# Mandatory Academic Thesis & Journal Guidelines

Whenever a user asks to draft, plan, review, or generate a **thesis (skripsi / tugas akhir)** or **academic journal article** in this repository, you **MUST** follow `.agents/rules/mandatory-academic-standards.md` and `.agents/skills/menulis-skripsi/SKILL.md` automatically:

1. **Topic Pipeline:** Reference analysis → **5 topic alternatives** → **exactly 1 best topic** → **justification (max 50 words)** + **summary (max ±250 words: problem, objectives, benefits, novelty, scope)** → systematic **outline**.
2. **Literature Protocol (MANDATORY):** English literature search questions per subsection; **≥5 references** per query; **Scopus Q1/Q2**; **English**; **Open Access** & **active (not discontinued)**; **2021–2026**; **≥5 citations**; **APA 7** `(Author et al., YEAR)` with **DOI**. Output must NOT list selected articles separately—combine citation statements into **1 complete 150-word English APA 7 paragraph** labeled `Citation:`, followed by the DOI reference list labeled `Bibliography:`.
3. **Word Counts & Substance (MANDATORY):**
   - **BAB I Pendahuluan:** ±1,500 words
   - **BAB II Tinjauan Pustaka:** ±2,500 words
   - **BAB III Metodologi Penelitian:** ±2,500 words
   - **BAB IV Hasil dan Pembahasan:** ±2,500 words
   - **BAB V Simpulan dan Saran:** ±750 words
   - **Abstrak & Kata Kunci:** ±250 words (1 paragraph) + ±5 keywords.
4. **Output Formatting:** Use `templates/formal_thesis_template.docx`, `templates/formal_thesis_template.pdf`, or `templates/TEMPLATE_SKRIPSI_DAN_JURNAL.md` (and `.agents/skills/skripsi-to-ieee/` for IEEE conversion). Never fabricate DOIs or results.
