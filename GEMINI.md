# Mandatory Academic Thesis & Journal Guidelines

Every request to create, outline, review, or convert a **thesis (skripsi / tugas akhir)** or **scientific journal paper** in this workspace MUST automatically enforce `.agents/rules/mandatory-academic-standards.md` and load `.agents/skills/menulis-skripsi/SKILL.md` (plus `.agents/skills/skripsi-to-ieee/SKILL.md` for IEEE papers):

1. **Topic Exploration & Framing:** Analyze primary reference → **5 topic alternatives** → **1 best topic** → **Justification (≤50 words)** + **Topic Summary (≤±250 words: problem, objectives, benefits, novelty, scope)** → **Systematic Outline**.
2. **Mandatory Literature Standard:** English search questions per subsection; **≥5 references** per search question; **Scopus Q1/Q2**; **English**; **Open Access** & active (**not discontinued**); **2021–2026**; **≥5 citations**; **APA 7** `(Author et al., YEAR)` + **DOI**. Output format MUST omit separate selected-article lists and present **`Citation:`** (1 unified 150-word English APA 7 paragraph) followed by **`Bibliography:`** (complete list with DOI).
3. **Mandatory Word Targets:** BAB I (±1,500 words), BAB II (±2,500 words), BAB III (±2,500 words), BAB IV (±2,500 words), BAB V (±750 words), Abstrak (±250 words in 1 paragraph + ±5 keywords).
4. **Master Templates:** Follow `templates/formal_thesis_template.docx`, `templates/formal_thesis_template.pdf`, and `templates/TEMPLATE_SKRIPSI_DAN_JURNAL.md`. Never fabricate citations, DOIs, or metrics.
