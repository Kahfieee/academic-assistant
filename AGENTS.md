# Mandatory Academic Thesis & Journal Guidelines

Whenever a user asks to draft, plan, review, or generate a **thesis (skripsi / tugas akhir)** or **academic journal article** in this repository, you **MUST** enforce all rules below automatically (and load `.agents/skills/menulis-skripsi/SKILL.md` or `.agents/skills/skripsi-to-ieee/SKILL.md` as appropriate):

## 1. Topic Exploration, Selection, Justification & Outline
- **Topic Exploration:** Analyze the primary reference article (focus, problem, method, data/object, findings, limitations, future opportunities) to propose **5 relevant and realistic topic alternatives**.
- **Topic Selection:** Select **exactly 1 best topic** aligned with the Study Program and Specialization, with a clear, realistic, and viable research problem.
- **Justification & Summary:**
  - **Justification:** maximum **50 words**.
  - **Topic Summary:** maximum **±250 words**, MUST include problem statement, objectives, benefits, novelty, and research scope.
- **Outline:** Structured systematically according to Study Program, Specialization, research topic, and chosen methodology.

## 2. Literature, Reference & Citation Standards (MANDATORY)
- **Search Language:** Formulate literature search questions for every subsection, sub-subsection, and key outline point in **English**.
- **Minimum Count:** At least **5 highly relevant references** per literature search question.
- **Journal Indexing:** Leading international scientific journals indexed in **Scopus Q1 or Scopus Q2**.
- **Reference Language:** **English**.
- **Access & Status:** **Open Access** and active (**not discontinued**).
- **Publication Window:** Last **5 years (2021–2026)**.
- **Citation Impact:** High impact, **at least 5 citations** from relevant scientific journals.
- **Citation & Bibliography Style:** **APA 7** in-text citations `(Author et al., YEAR)` and full bibliography entries **with official DOI**.
- **Synthesis Output Format:** Combine all citation statements in English into **1 unified 150-word paragraph** in APA 7 format prefixed with **`Citation:`**, followed by the complete DOI-included reference list prefixed with **`Bibliography:`**, **without listing selected articles separately** (*Do not write selected articles*).

## 3. Manuscript Content & Word-Count Targets (MANDATORY)
- **BAB I Pendahuluan:** **±1,500 words** — Follow outline, use `(Author et al., YEAR)` citations, maintain continuity across problem, objectives, contribution, and scope.
- **BAB II Tinjauan Pustaka:** **±2,500 words** — Synthesize concepts, theories, literature evolution, prior studies, research gaps, and alignment with the present study.
- **BAB III Metodologi Penelitian:** **±2,500 words** — Operational and systematic; link research questions, objectives, data, methods, experiments, testing, and evaluation.
- **BAB IV Hasil dan Pembahasan:** **±2,500 words** — Present objective results (supported by data/tables/charts/confusion matrix), interpret findings, answer objectives, and compare with prior studies.
- **BAB V Simpulan dan Saran:** **±750 words** — Conclusions directly answer objectives and research questions and state meaning/contributions; recommendations address follow-up and future research.
- **Abstrak & Kata Kunci:** **±250 words (1 paragraph)** — Cover problem, objective, method, main results, interpretation, conclusion, and implications; include **±5 keywords**.

## 4. Scientific Integrity
Never fabricate citations, DOIs, datasets, sample counts, or experimental metrics. Always verify against real literature and user-provided experimental outputs. Master output templates reside in `templates/`.
